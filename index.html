Test checklist for two testers

Allow about 20 minutes. One person plays the operator (Demo Mine C) and one plays the regulator. Each person opens this page on their own device. Readings are simulated and each device runs its own session, so sit together and compare screens.

Operator tester

  1. Open Operator view and choose Demo Mine C, Mufulira.
  2. Click a few parameter rows and check that the chart and its dashed limit line change.
  3. Press Simulate a breach and wait until a status label shows Exceeds limit.
  4. In the events table press Acknowledge on the new event.
  5. Press Log action, type a corrective action and press Save.
  6. Wait up to one minute. Once the reading is back within its limit for 3 readings in a row, the event should change to Closed.
  7. Press Pause feed, then Resume feed.

Regulator tester

  1. Open Regulator view and note which operators show red, amber or green on the map.
  2. Find the most recent event in the exceedance feed and read its peak and limit.
  3. Press Request inspection for one operator, then open Operator view for that operator and check the inspection note.
  4. Open Limit register, change the copper maximum to 0.5 and watch new exceedances appear.
  5. Open Compliance report, choose an operator, press Print or save as PDF and Download CSV.
